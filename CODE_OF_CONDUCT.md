# Code of conduct

The porthole-dev organization adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for every repository and every space it runs (issues,
pull requests, discussions).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep criticism
about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing
someone's private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to jertlok@proton.me. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions
that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants who do,
following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
